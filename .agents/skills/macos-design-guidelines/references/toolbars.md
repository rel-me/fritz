# Toolbars

Prioritize actions needed for the current workflow. Check labels, grouping,
enabled state, overflow, and availability when the window narrows. Icon-only
items need meaningful accessibility names and discoverable intent.

Preserve standard window controls and existing toolbar materials. Customization,
search, and segmented controls are useful when the surface supports those tasks;
their absence alone is not a finding. Keep toolbar commands reachable through
appropriate menus/keyboard access, and verify they target the focused window or
selected record. Reuse [menu](menus.md) and [accessibility](accessibility.md)
guidance rather than repeating those checklists.
