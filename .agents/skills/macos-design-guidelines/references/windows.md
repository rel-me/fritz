# Windows

Evaluate the affected window's actual role: chat workspace, provider window,
settings, or transient editor. Preserve its established scene and ownership model.
A missing extra window, document proxy icon, or fullscreen mode is not automatically
a defect in a surface that does not offer that workflow.

Check usable resizing, minimum size, content clipping, focus, and restoration
where supported. Verify close/minimize/zoom and draggable regions remain usable
when custom chrome changes. Do not prescribe removal of custom chrome or addition
of multiwindow/document behavior without a user-facing problem.

For window state, test reopening and switching projects/threads/windows. Report what was
observed; a scene declaration or default position alone does not prove restoration.
