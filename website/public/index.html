What do I need to get started?

A Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 15 or later. Download Fritz, open the disk image, and drag the app to Applications. Add a provider in Settings with your own API key, or choose Fritz to download a local model. Provider usage may have its own costs; local models need enough memory and disk space. Read the setup guide.

What’s the difference between Chat and Code?

Talk through ideas in Chat without changing your files. In Code, Fritz can read and edit project files and run commands. Use Code with projects you trust: it has the same access to your Mac that you do. Downloaded Fritz models currently support Chat. Explore Code mode.

Where do my conversations and keys live?

Fritz saves your conversations and project settings on your Mac, and keeps your provider keys securely on your Mac. When you use a remote provider, your prompts and relevant context are sent to that provider. Downloaded Fritz models can chat offline after setup.
