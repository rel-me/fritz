# Fritz identity

The rounded F is the app mark. Its coral middle stroke suggests a conversation. The dark teal tile is the Dock icon; `FritzLogo.svg` and `FritzLogoDark.svg` are horizontal wordmarks for light and dark backgrounds.

The three numbered SVGs are the editable vector source for the Dock icon. `FritzMark.svg` and `FritzMarkDark.svg` supply the mark in light and dark chat appearances. `app/Resources/AppIcon.icon` is the Icon Composer document used by Xcode. After changing the numbered vectors, export a 1024 × 1024 opaque image from them and replace `Assets/FritzIcon.png` in the icon document. Keep the source layers square and let the system mask the icon corners.
